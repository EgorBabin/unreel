# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-15

First release.

### Browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Firefox)
- Blocks the Reels feed (`/reels/`) and every reel that is not yours.
- Blocks other people's profile **Reels** tab, sending you to their regular grid instead.
- Keeps your own reels (including ones you co-posted) watchable and editable.
- Detects your username automatically; optional manual override.
- Hides Reels buttons, shows a “checking…” screen so no reel ever plays, redirect to Home or your profile.
- Blocked-reels counter and a polished dark popup.

### Android app
- Accessibility service that closes the Reels tab and other people's reels in the Instagram app.
- Your own reels stay open; username detected from your profile tab or set manually.
- No internet permission, no tracking.
