# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

**Unreel collects nothing.** No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, no servers.

## Browser extension

- Runs only on `instagram.com`.
- Stores four things in your browser's local extension storage: whether protection is on, your username (typed or detected), your preferences, and a counter of blocked reels.
- To tell whether a reel is yours, it asks **Instagram itself** (the same `instagram.com` API the website already uses, with your existing session) who posted that reel. That request goes only to Instagram — never to us or anyone else.
- It does not read your messages, your feed content, your contacts or your password.

## Android app

- Has **no internet permission** — it physically cannot send data anywhere.
- Its accessibility service is limited to the Instagram app (`com.instagram.android`). It reads only the on-screen labels needed to recognise the Reels player and the name of the reel's author.
- Stores the same settings and counter as the extension, privately on your phone.
